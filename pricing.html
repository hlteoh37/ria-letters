Frequently Asked Questions

Are the templates really compliance-aware?

Yes. Every template is written with SEC marketing rule guidance in mind — they avoid client testimonials, performance guarantees, and language that could be seen as misleading. That said, you should always have your compliance officer review any communication before sending, as rules vary by firm and jurisdiction.

How are these different from generic ChatGPT output?

Generic AI-generated letters don't know the specific language patterns, disclosures, and tone that clients of financial advisors expect — and they have no awareness of SEC marketing rules. Our templates are purpose-built for RIAs: professional, advisor-specific, and reviewed for compliance-sensitive language.

Can I customize the templates for my firm?

Absolutely. All templates are structured with clear placeholders and sections. You can add your firm's name, logo in your email client, specific disclosures, and any personal touches that reflect your practice's voice.

What happens after my 7-day free trial?

How often are new templates added?

We add new templates regularly based on advisor requests and changes in the regulatory environment. All new templates are included in your subscription at no extra cost.

Do I need any special software?

No. Templates are delivered as clean, editable documents. You can open them in Word, Google Docs, or paste directly into your email client. No special software required.
