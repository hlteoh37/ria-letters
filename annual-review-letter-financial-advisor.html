Annual Review Letter for Financial Advisors: Templates & Examples

The annual review letter is your single most important scheduled client communication. It bridges the formal annual review meeting (or replaces it for smaller accounts) and gives every client a clear narrative of where they stand, what changed, and what comes next. A well-executed annual review letter reinforces your value proposition, surfaces planning opportunities, and dramatically reduces client attrition.

What an Annual Review Letter Should Accomplish

The annual review letter serves four distinct purposes: relationship maintenance (the client hears from you at a meaningful touchpoint), progress reporting (goals are tracked against plan), planning triggers (life changes and new opportunities are surfaced), and retention insurance (clients who feel informed stay longer and refer more). A letter that accomplishes all four creates compounding relationship value over time.

Structure of a High-Performing Annual Review Letter

  1. Year-in-review summary — briefly contextualize markets, the economy, and any personal milestones
  2. Goal progress check — map portfolio/plan status against the client's specific goals
  3. Key actions taken — highlight 2–3 things you did for the client during the year
  4. Planning agenda for the coming year — what topics should be on the agenda for the next meeting
  5. Life-change check-in — prompt the client to share any changes in income, family, health, or goals
  6. Invitation to meet — clear CTA to schedule the annual review meeting

Sample Annual Review Letter

Dear [Client Name],

As we close out [Year], I wanted to take a moment to reflect on the year together and look ahead to what we want to accomplish in [Next Year].

Where We Stand: Your portfolio ended the year at approximately [value], [up/down X%] for the year. In the context of [brief market summary], this result reflects your [conservative/balanced/growth] posture and our decision to [key action, e.g., add international exposure in Q2].

Progress Toward Your Goals: You are [on track/ahead of/slightly behind] your [retirement/education/income] goal. [One sentence on what that means practically].

Looking Ahead: In our next meeting, I'd like to cover [topic 1], [topic 2], and revisit your [beneficiary designations / estate plan / insurance coverage]. Has anything changed in your life — family, income, or goals — that I should know about?

I'd love to schedule our annual review in [month]. You can book directly at [scheduling link] or reply to this email.

Thank you for another year of trust.
Warmly,
[Advisor Name]

Compliance Note

Annual review letters that reference performance must comply with your firm's performance presentation policies and applicable SEC advertising rules. Avoid specific return claims unless you've followed compliant performance presentation standards. Use descriptive language ("broadly positive year for equity markets") when discussing markets rather than specific benchmark comparisons that could create compliance issues.
